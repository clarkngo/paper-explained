Attention Is All You Need

Major Impacts

  • Foundation for modern NLP: Transformers became the base architecture for BERT, GPT, and most current language models.
  • Stronger sequence modeling: Self-attention improves long-range dependency handling compared to recurrent models.
  • Parallel training: Removing recurrence allows significantly faster training on modern hardware.
  • Cross-domain influence: The architecture expanded into vision, speech, and multimodal systems.

ELI5

Imagine reading a sentence and highlighting the most important words to understand it. Transformers teach computers to do that for every word at once, so they can understand meaning faster and better.

Explained to New Devs

Before transformers, models like RNNs read text token-by-token. This was slow and made long context hard to retain. The transformer processes all tokens together and uses self-attention to decide which other tokens matter for each token. That shift made training more efficient and quality better.

Explained to Senior Devs

The paper proposes a purely attention-based encoder-decoder architecture with multi-head self-attention, feed-forward blocks, residuals, normalization, and positional encodings. Removing recurrence improves compute parallelism while preserving expressive power for sequence transduction. Its core contribution is an architecture-level simplification that also scales favorably with data and model size.
